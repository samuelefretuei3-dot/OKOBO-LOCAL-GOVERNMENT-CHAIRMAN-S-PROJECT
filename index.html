ABOUT THIS MILESTONE

Celebrating Two Years of Leadership

Recognising a milestone of service, commitment and dedication to the people of Okobo Local Government Area.

Leadership Through Service

Leadership is measured not only by the position one occupies, but also by the positive difference made in the lives of the people. This celebration recognises two years of service and commitment to the development of Okobo.

We celebrate the efforts towards community development, improved public services, unity and a brighter future for the people of Okobo Local Government Area.

A SPECIAL CONGRATULATORY MESSAGE

Two Years of Service & Leadership

Rt. Hon. (Engr.) George Henshew

Executive Chairman

Okobo Local Government Area

On behalf of the good people of Okobo Local Government Area, Akwa Ibom State, we warmly congratulate Rt. Hon. (Engr.) George Henshew on the successful completion of two years in office.

Your leadership, dedication and commitment to the development of our people have contributed to community progress, improved public services and renewed hope across the local government.

As you mark this important milestone, we pray for greater wisdom, strength, good health and continued success in the years ahead.

Two Years of Service and Impact

A celebration of the values and development priorities that contribute to a stronger community.

Good Governance

Strengthening institutions, encouraging accountability and promoting responsible leadership.

CBT Centre Construction

Construction of a Computer-Based Testing centre to support digital examinations and educational opportunities.

Youth Empowerment Programme

Sending youths to participate in an empowerment training programme that is currently ongoing.

People Focused

Encouraging community participation and improving access to essential public services.

Infrastructure

Supporting the development of roads, drainage systems, public buildings and other vital facilities.

Unity & Peace

Promoting harmony, security and a stronger, more united Okobo community.
